The manifest confirms the brief's count of 129 images.

⚠️ **Only 85 of the 129 images are unique.** 44 PNG pages appear twice under names like
`…-07__1O_M-H0E7z.png` and `…-07__1qaWGv8qLJ.png`, with byte-identical sha256. The suffix looks
like a Google Drive file-ID artefact from the download. Unique content:
- **80 PNGs**: one render of each of the 80 PDF pages.
- **5 JPGs**: phone photos of a real paper booklet (see §4).

Any accuracy number must count each unique page once, or it will double-weight pages 7, 10, 12, 14–42 and 44–56.

**Naming pattern**
- `dossiers_specimen_10_patientes-NN[__<driveid>].png`: NN is the PDF page number (01–80).
  Patient = `(NN-1)//8 + 1`, and the page within the record = `(NN-1)%8 + 1`. The filename reveals the patient
  and the section. The language is always French.
- `1-K.jpg`: one booklet photographed page by page (K = 1..5). It has no link to the PDF patients.

**Pages per patient.** There are 10 patients with 8 pages each, always in this order:

| Page in record | Section (printed title) |
|---|---|
| 1 | FICHE DE SURVEILLANCE… (cover: facility, risk) |
| 2 | IDENTIFICATION ET ANTÉCÉDENTS |
| 3 | GROSSESSE ACTUELLE (9 visit columns) |
| 4 | DÉROULEMENT DE L'ACCOUCHEMENT |
| 5 | POST-PARTUM PRÉCOCE: MÈRE |
| 6 | POST-PARTUM PRÉCOCE: NOUVEAU-NÉ |
| 7 | POST-PARTUM TARDIF: MÈRE |
| 8 | POST-PARTUM TARDIF: NOUVEAU-NÉ |

**Ground truth files.** ⚠️ **None were provided.** The brief promises "ground-truth values for each page", but
there is no JSON or CSV per page in the folder or the manifest. The CSV is not ground truth for the images (see §5).

**Workaround (found during inspection).** The PDF is vector data, so the ground truth can be recovered exactly:
- Printed labels use Helvetica.
- "Handwriting" is drawn one glyph at a time with small random rotations. Each patient has one handwriting
  font: Caveat, ShadowsIntoLight, NanumPen, Gaegu or ReenieBeanie, each used for 2 patients. Every font has a
  ToUnicode map, so the text decodes exactly.
- Checkboxes are 8×8 pt rectangles. A mark is a set of ink-coloured strokes inside the rectangle.

`tools/pdf_ground_truth.py` extracts these elements for each page:
- 2,112 handwritten strings
- 1,970 checkboxes, 478 of them checked
- a label and a mark style for each checkbox
- pixel coordinates in the PNG for each element

It redacts identifiers before writing anything. The output is a raw draft: the strings still have to be
mapped to schema fields using table geometry (row label by y, column header by x).

**Image ↔ CSV id mapping:** no. **Several images per patient:** yes, 8 pages × 10 patients
(1 to 2 files per page because of the duplicates).

## 3. Ground truth (derived from the PDF vector layer)

**Fields in ground truth vs PDF.** They are the same document, so every field is covered. The only gap is
that the ground truth does not say which field a string belongs to. That mapping comes from geometry.

**How the ground truth encodes missing or special values:**

| Seen on page | Count | Meaning | Proposed status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Empty cell / empty line (no string) | many | nothing written | `NOT_PROVIDED` |
| `—` (em dash) | 129, all on P3 | deliberately not done / not applicable (e.g. fetal movements at 12 SA, no appointment after the 9th month) | `NOT_APPLICABLE` (keep raw `—`) |
| `RAS` | 216 | "rien à signaler" = normal | `KNOWN`, value `RAS` |
| `Néant`, `Aucun(e)` | 60 | none | `KNOWN` |
| `Non fait` | 4 | test not done | `KNOWN` (value "not done") |
| `Neg`, `Immune`, `Oui`/`Non`, `Normales`, `Normaux` | 148 / 15 / 163 / 102 | categorical | `KNOWN` |
| Illegible marker, `?`, crossed-out correction | **0** | n/a | ⚠️ never occurs in PDF data |

⚠️ **ILLEGIBLE and UNKNOWN cannot be evaluated on the provided data.** No crossed-out values exist
(0 ink strokes outside checkboxes). Those statuses will only come from the model's own uncertainty, from the
real JPGs, or from test pages we degrade or generate ourselves.

⚠️ **Missing glyphs.** The NanumPen and Gaegu fonts (patients 3, 4, 8 and 9) have no accented letters.
The ground truth says `Collège` and `Couturière`, but the image shows `coll  ge` and `couturi  re` with blank
gaps (152 strings affected). Comparison must be accent- and whitespace-insensitive, or these pages will be
scored unfairly.

**Formats used:**
- **Dates:** `DD/MM/YYYY` (249 occurrences). `YYYY-NNN-NNN` is the fiche number, not a date.
- **Gestational age:** `NN SA` (e.g. `31 SA`).
- **BP:** `SSS/DD` in mmHg, no unit (e.g. `104/74`).
- **Units written inline on single fields:** `3587 g`, `34 cm`, `37.1 °C`, `66 kg`, `11.8 g/dL`, `0.93 g/L`, `186k` (platelets), `7 jours`.
- **Table cells:** bare numbers (`58.8`, `150`).
- **Decimal separator:** `.`.
- **Booleans:** checkboxes, or `Oui`/`Non`.
- **Sex:** `F`/`M`.

**Granularity:** per page, and the pages group into a patient record.

**Identifiers in the ground truth:** yes. Name ×3, CIN, address, phone and husband's name are all in the PDF.
The tool replaces them with `[REDACTED]` and keeps a `field_hint`. The pipeline must ignore them, and
fixtures must never contain them.

**The 5 JPGs (viewed individually).** They show a **real printed Ministry of Health booklet** (pink cover,
Arabic + French letterhead) filled in by hand in blue ballpoint.

- **1-1 (cover).**
  - Arabic appears only in the printed letterhead.
  - "Fixe" is ticked.
  - The name line is physically covered with a paper strip.
  - A handwritten fiche/ID-like number is still visible.
  - The photo has mild perspective and a dark background.
- **1-2 (identification + history).**
  - Age is written `21ans` (unit attached).
  - A CIN-like number and an address are handwritten. ⚠️ They are visible and unredacted.
  - A single large diagonal `RAS` spans a whole column of rows, so one mark answers 5 cells.
  - Strong perspective; a finger is in the frame.
- **1-3 (obstetric history + previous deliveries).**
  - Diagonal `RAS` again, plus slash strokes through empty cells.
  - Gestation, parity and living-children values are ambiguous scribbles.
  - The checkboxes are empty.
- **1-4 (current pregnancy, 1st trimester).**
  - Blood group and Rh are marked by **circling the printed letter**, a mark style absent from the PDF.
  - Dates use 2-digit years: `DD/MM/YY`.
  - GA is written `16SA+3j` (weeks + days).
  - ⚠️ **BP is written in cmHg (`11/7`)**, not mmHg.
  - Decimals use a comma: `0,76g/L`. Platelets are written `233000/mm3`.
  - Contains `nég`, `Test rapide` and long diagonal "nothing" strokes.
  - Text overflows cells. Examiners are given as initials.
- **1-5 (2nd/3rd trimester, facing page).**
  - Dates are `D/M/YY` (`20/1/26`) and weights `63Kg`.
  - Free-text notes run across 4 cells.
  - The left column is cut off by the page fold.

These real pages differ from the PDF template in layout:
- "Grossesse actuelle" is split across two facing pages.
- There are extra rows (`Autres`, `Autres à préciser`).
- The cover has a "Mode de la couverture" block.
- The PDF ground truth does not cover them.

**Arabic.** ⚠️ The 85 unique images contain **no Arabic handwriting and no Eastern Arabic digits**
(confirmed by the decoded text: zero Arabic code points). The only Arabic is the printed letterhead in
`1-1.jpg`. The 30-point criterion mentions Arabic, but we cannot measure it with the provided data.

**Hardest cases:** 1-4 and 1-5 (dense cursive, cmHg, ranges, overflow), the diagonal "RAS spans many cells" in
1-2 and 1-3, the gaps from missing accents (patients 3/4/8/9), and the scribble marks (patients 4 and 8) that
look like strike-through.
